Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to WriteBee. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information required to provide a functional and personalized educational experience:

  • Automatically Collected Data: Anonymous device identifiers used solely to save your progress (like unlocked levels) and manage purchase statuses.
  • We Do NOT Collect: Names, emails, phone numbers, location data, or any directly identifiable personal information.

3. How We Use Information

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Save and sync your learning progress across sessions.
  • Manage in-app purchases and unlock premium content.
  • Provide child-safe, non-personalized advertisements (for free-tier users).
  • Improve app stability by analyzing anonymous crash logs.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party service providers for cloud storage, payment processing, and safe advertising. These partners only receive the anonymous data necessary to perform their specific functions and are configured to operate in child-safe modes.

5. Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide app functionality or as required by law. You may request data deletion at any time.
