1. Introduction

Welcome to Write Bee (the "App"), a mobile educational application developed by Dhruvil Mangukiya and provided by Alphaved Private Limited ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Because our App is an educational tool designed for children, we take privacy very seriously and are fully committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other applicable international privacy laws.

2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information (such as names, addresses, or email addresses) from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately so we can promptly securely delete it.

B. Non-Personal & Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, we or our third-party service providers may automatically collect certain non-personal information solely to improve the App's performance and provide a better experience. This may include:

  • Device information (e.g., device model, operating system version)
  • App usage data (e.g., time spent on levels, feature usage, educational progress)
  • Anonymous crash reports and diagnostic data

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate, maintain, and improve our App. These services are bound by their own privacy policies and are heavily restricted from using children's data for targeted advertising. The services we use may include:

  • Firebase / Google Analytics: Used for analytics, crash reporting, and securely saving game progress. Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: Used to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions securely. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Unity: The game engine used to build the App. Unity Privacy Policy

4. In-App Purchases

Our App may offer in-app purchases (e.g., unlocking new levels or premium features). All transactions are securely processed directly through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not collect, process, or store your payment information on our servers.

5. Security of Your Information

We use industry-standard administrative, logical, and physical security measures to protect your information. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
